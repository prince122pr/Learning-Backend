# Large Language Models (LLMs) Notes

Large Language Models (LLMs) are **artificial intelligence models**
trained on huge amounts of text data to understand and generate
human-like language.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🔑 Core Concepts

### 1. Definition

-   LLMs are neural networks trained to predict the next word in a
    sequence.
-   They learn grammar, facts, reasoning patterns, and context from
    large text datasets.

### 2. Architecture

-   Most LLMs are based on the **Transformer architecture** (introduced
    in 2017).
-   Key components:
    -   **Attention mechanism**: Helps model focus on relevant words in
        context.
    -   **Encoder / Decoder blocks**: For processing sequences of text.

### 3. Training

-   Trained on billions of tokens (words/pieces of text).
-   Objective: Minimize prediction error for the next token.

### 4. Parameters

-   LLMs are defined by the number of parameters (weights in the
    network).
-   Example: GPT-3 has **175 billion parameters**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ⚙️ Examples of Popular LLMs

-   **GPT (OpenAI)** -- ChatGPT is based on this.
-   **Gemini (Google DeepMind)**.
-   **Claude (Anthropic)**.
-   **LLaMA (Meta)**.
-   **Mistral, Falcon** -- Open-source models.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🔥 Capabilities

-   Text generation (chat, stories, code).
-   Translation and summarization.
-   Question answering.
-   Sentiment analysis.
-   Reasoning and problem-solving.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ⚠️ Limitations

-   Can produce **hallucinations** (confident but wrong answers).
-   Dependent on training data (bias, outdated info).
-   Computationally expensive (requires GPUs/TPUs).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📌 Applications

-   Chatbots and virtual assistants.
-   Content creation (blogs, marketing copy).
-   Customer support automation.
-   Code generation and debugging.
-   Research and data analysis.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📌 Summary

LLMs are **powerful AI models** capable of understanding and generating
natural language.\
They are the foundation of modern AI applications, but must be combined
with **tools, databases, and frameworks (like LangChain)** to be more
reliable and practical.
